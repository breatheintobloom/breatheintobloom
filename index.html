Why it matters

Most people never learn how their body works, until something isn't working.

Fertility, cycles, and hormones shape how people feel and function every day, yet most of what people know comes from school health class and the internet. When fertility questions or treatment come up, they're navigating it alongside a full-time job, often with no clear information and no tools.

1 in 6

people worldwide are affected by infertility at some point in their lives.

World Health Organization, 2023. Source
